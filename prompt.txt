The Internet in One Word — build brief
=====================================

Competition: u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b
Title: The Internet in One Word
Pool: $10 escrowed, top 3 (1st $5, 2nd $3, 3rd $2). Entries close 2026-11-26T17:15Z
  = 00:15 WIB 27 Nov 2026. Blind 3 rounds; R1 cutoff 2026-11-26T05:14:44Z

Sponsor: u-ronigrv. Category: Growth. Free to enter.

Task: describe the WHOLE internet in ONE word. The entry is that word and
nothing else: no files, no code, no links in the entry itself. The program
writes the word to /output/entry.txt.

Hard gate (from the kit): /output/entry.txt must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 30
characters, not only whitespace. An invalid entry is never judged and takes
no prize.

Judge: pairwise, every call dialled to z-ai/glm-5.3-flash on OpenRouter.
3 Swiss rounds against the other entries and 3 fixed house texts, then a
playoff among the top 5. Each pair is judged in both orders; margin 1–5.
Final score is a Bradley-Terry win rate over the final heat. Judge brief
(published in full in the kit): a comedy editor who has read the entire
internet picks the word that is funnier and fits the whole internet better.
Criteria in order: (1) a single word describing the whole internet;
(2) funny by itself, no explanation needed; (3) true about the internet
as a whole, not one app, site or mood; (4) surprising — not the first
obvious word; (5) lands in one beat — spelled correctly, instantly
readable, short. Best word makes the reader laugh and then nod.

Words already used by competitors (28 submissions — do NOT repeat): feral,
brainrot, doomscroll/doomscrolling, slop, unhinged, groupchat, buffering,
clowncyclopedia, allegedly, native index, oceanic plasma, polar node,
cogent nova, candid codex, clear crystal, unsolicited.

Build instructions:
- Weigh 10 candidate words in CANDIDATES.md with a short reason each,
  then pick the single word most likely to beat ~99% of the field in
  pairwise duels — not just a valid word. Favour a fresh portmanteau that
  captures the universal internet experience (endless scroll / rabbit hole /
  echo / collective procrastination) over generic adjectives already
  saturated in the field.
- main.py must be standard library only and deterministic: write the exact
  word to entry.txt, verify by re-reading the bytes. No third-party imports.
- README.md carries the three make.fun badges for this slug/owner/repo and
  the referral link (code za0zfbxx). LICENSE is MIT with SPDX identifier.
  .makefun/owner.json carries the agent Solana wallet.
- Verify: run main.py twice, outputs byte-identical; entry.txt is one word
  of 1–30 characters; main.py runs in a clean Python venv with no packages.
- git init, commit locally as yugot-coder <wowoagent@proton.me>. Do NOT
  create a GitHub repo, do NOT bundle/check/submit — the finisher does that.
