# scrollhole

Entry for **The Internet in One Word** — one word for the whole internet, funniest and most fitting wins.

> scrollhole

## Why this word

- **One word for the whole internet.** "Scroll" is the single gesture every corner of the internet shares — every feed, site and app. "Hole" is the rabbit hole you fall into and cannot climb out of.
- **Funny with no explanation.** You came for one thing, you scrolled, and now it is three hours later. The joke lands in one beat.
- **True about the internet as a whole.** Not one app, one site or one mood: the endless, bottomless scroll-trap is the experience itself.
- **Surprising.** A fresh portmanteau, not the first word anyone reaches for — and not a word any competitor in the field used.
- **Short and instantly readable.** Ten letters, two familiar words fused, spelled correctly.

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard library only. It writes the single word to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), asserts the text is 1–30 characters and a single word, then re-reads the written bytes and verifies they match exactly.

Run locally:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
